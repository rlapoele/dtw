## Independently checked claims

- **Precision and policy:** both inspected implementations use Number/native Math. The report correctly separates exact canonical editing, identity, alpha and decimal byte adaptation from approximate cross-space arithmetic and output formatting. Twelve significant digits are not twelve accurate digits. Higher-precision arithmetic cannot be inserted around an unchanged Number calculation to recover lost distinctions. Direct arbitrary-precision output would require reviewing the agreed binary-result rounding basis; coefficient provenance and nonlinear uncertainty remain separate concerns.
- **Missing and neutral behavior:** installed Color.js `lch.fromBase` contains the near-neutral axis threshold inherited by OKLCH. Pinned Culori [polar conversion](https://github.com/Evercoder/culori/blob/v4.0.2/src/lch/convertLabToLch.js) instead tests computed chroma, defaults missing axes to zero and uses `sqrt(a*a+b*b)`. Its [RGB-to-OKLAB wrapper](https://github.com/Evercoder/culori/blob/v4.0.2/src/oklab/convertRgbToOklab.js) zeroes axes after numeric RGB equality. These facts support the stated policy-adapter work and Culori source advantage, without proving runtime near-neutral accuracy or exact authored equality after Number conversion.
- **Actual-route observability:** installed Color.js `ColorSpace`, `RGBColorSpace`, HSL, OKLAB and conversion sources, and pinned Culori [converter](https://github.com/Evercoder/culori/blob/v4.0.2/src/converter.js) and [linear-RGB-to-OKLAB](https://github.com/Evercoder/culori/blob/v4.0.2/src/oklab/convertLrgbToOklab.js), support the distinction between composable public stages and inaccessible inline/nonlinear work. Extension points do not supply a replacement arithmetic backend for built-in transforms. An endpoint wrapper or shadow trace does not establish enforcement on the actual calculation. The comparison preserves the unagreed stage-granularity and conservative-refusal questions.
- **Extended output and serializers:** raw Color.js `.to()` only requests gamut mapping through its optional setting; its HSL implementation includes extended-color handling. Culori's pinned [HSL source](https://github.com/Evercoder/culori/blob/v4.0.2/src/hsl/convertRgbToHsl.js) differs, while its [formatter](https://github.com/Evercoder/culori/blob/v4.0.2/src/formatter.js) confirms RGB/HEX clamping and rounding. The report appropriately requires constructor/extended-boundary fixtures rather than treating coverage or metadata as equivalence.
- **Future manipulation:** installed Color.js interpolation source and its [official guidance](https://colorjs.io/docs/interpolation) confirm two-endpoint mixing/ranges, discrete steps, hue selection and optional premultiplication. Its actual range path unconditionally calls `toGamut` on converted endpoints; the report correctly limits the consequence to endpoints that mapping changes. Pinned Culori [interpolation](https://github.com/Evercoder/culori/blob/v4.0.2/src/interpolate/interpolate.js), [piecewise interpolation](https://github.com/Evercoder/culori/blob/v4.0.2/src/interpolate/piecewise.js) and [polar definition](https://github.com/Evercoder/culori/blob/v4.0.2/src/lch/definition.js) substantiate positioned stops, hints/easing, parameter clamping, separate premultiplied interpolation and the stated missing/hue behavior. No corresponding gamut call appears in that interpolation path. These capabilities justify examining reuse, not claiming complete CSS-gradient or DTW-gradient semantics.
- **Provenance and maintenance:** the installed Color.js manifest reports `0.7.1`; its official `v0.7.1` tag manifest reports `0.7.0`, as disclosed. The pinned Culori manifest reports `4.0.2`. Both manifests declare MIT and omit runtime dependencies. The maintenance comparison uses identifiable ownership/verification tasks and acknowledges dual-route seam costs; it supplies no invented days, bundle measurements, performance ranking or maintainer guarantees.

The minor attribution issue noticed in the superseded draft—assigning matrix exposure to `ColorSpace` rather than `RGBColorSpace` and individual modules—is corrected in this reviewed snapshot. It does not affect the recommendation.
